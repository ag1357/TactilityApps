Change: core/viewport.c ct_viewport_scale_stride — exact incremental floor mapping.
Per row, the source row base is computed once ((y*render_h/h)*render_w, one division);
per destination pixel the column index steps by add/compare/subtract with
rem = x*render_w mod w, which is exactly the direct x*render_w/w floor division
for all nonnegative operands. No per-pixel 64-bit division remains. Destination
pairs are written with one memcpy'd 32-bit word when dst is 4-byte aligned and
stride is even (little-endian paired halves; same values); otherwise the scalar
division-free loop runs. Output indices are identical to the previous direct form.
